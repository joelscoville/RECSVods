[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

"Archive content" means the metadata and annotations written for this project to describe
recordings: service and chapter titles, chapter times and types, summaries, keywords, topics,
series labels, the Bible references listed for each chapter, and the
project-authored parts of internal records (for example passage titles, summaries and
review notes), in `services/`, `taxonomy/`, `evaluation/` and similar data files.

retained records in `transcripts/` contain; see below.

## How to credit

Credit **"RECS Replay contributors"** with a link to this repository and to the licence, for
example:

> Chapter data from RECS Replay contributors, <repository link>, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

Individual contributors are listed in the repository's Git history. If you adapt the content,
you must say so and share your adapted version under CC BY-SA 4.0.
